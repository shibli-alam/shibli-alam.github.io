Current work

Research

Wake dynamics of a singly oscillating airfoil

Most unsteady-aerodynamics work studies airfoils oscillating continuously, where every cycle is contaminated by the wake of the one before it. I isolate a single pitching cycle instead, which separates the vorticity generated by the motion itself from the history of the flow. Across more than twenty kinematic cases spanning Strouhal number and reduced frequency, I quantify peak vorticity, circulation, core radius, and convection speed — and find that pitch amplitude, not frequency, is what governs how strong the shed vortices are.
